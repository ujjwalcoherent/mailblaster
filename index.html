Section 1 — Gmail accounts

Add one account per Gmail address you send from. Each keeps its own credentials, send progress and Stop button — starting a campaign on one account never touches another, so several can send at once in this tab.

Editing — new account —

Use a Google App Password (16 characters), not your normal password. Enable 2-Step Verification, then create one at myaccount.google.com → Security → App passwords.

Advanced SMTP port, auto-scan, remember on this browser
Deployment API key not required here

A hosted deployment sets MAILBLASTER_API_KEY so its campaign history cannot be read or deleted by anyone who knows the URL. Paste that value here. It is stored in this browser only, and sent only to this site.

Section 2 — Reconstruct a past campaign

Find a campaign you already sent — before this tool, in another tool, or one that didn't get logged here — directly from a saved account's Sent folder, so a follow-up can thread onto the real conversation instead of arriving as a new, unrelated email. Search by subject text, the recipient's email, a date range, or any combination; leave everything blank to just browse by date. Do this before Section 3 (Recipients) if the people you're about to email are the same ones from a campaign you want to pick up from.

Paste one of the emails instead uses its subject/Message-Id to seed the search

Section 3 — Recipients & Salutations

Paste email IDs in any format — commas, spaces, new lines, or copied from a sheet. Names are parsed from the email ID itself and used as {{name}}.
